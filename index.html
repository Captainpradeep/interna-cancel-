Frequently asked questions

Helpful Things to Know

Are you an official airline?

No. This is an independent third-party travel assistance service and is not the official website, call center, or representative of any airline.

Can you guarantee a refund or free cancellation?

No. Refunds, credits, fees, and cancellation eligibility depend on the airline, ticket terms, applicable law, and the facts of the booking.

Will there be a service fee?

Fees, if any, must be explained before you authorize a paid service. Ask the representative to confirm the total cost and scope of service before proceeding.

Can I contact the airline directly instead?

Yes. You may always contact the airline or the travel agency that issued your ticket directly, especially for official booking changes and confirmation.

What should I have ready?

It may help to have your airline name, travel date, and booking reference available. Do not share payment card details or sensitive information unless you understand why it is required and trust the channel.
